How to play

You’re Alex Morgan at fictional company Northbridge. Each case includes a realistic app screen and four evidence checks. Use the checks before deciding whether the request can proceed as shown.

Some requests are genuine. A familiar name, real app or polished message isn’t enough on its own. Verification and normal organisational controls matter.

All contacts, records and actions here are simulated. No calls are made, messages sent, files downloaded or clipboard changes performed.
