What to take away

  • A familiar logo or sender name is not proof of identity.
  • A genuine service can carry a malicious request or file.
  • A genuine sign-in page can authorise an unwanted device session. Check which process you initiated.
  • Do not run commands just because a document preview asks you to repair or verify something.
  • HTTPS protects a connection; it does not make a site trustworthy.
  • Never share a sign-in code with an unexpected contact or grant remote control on their say-so.
  • Preview QR destinations and verify requests through a route you already trust.
