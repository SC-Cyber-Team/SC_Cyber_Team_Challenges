    How this attempt worked

    An expected invoice created the opening. Conversation history and project details supplied credibility. The bank-change request introduced urgency, the document added a sign-in lure, and the Teams contact offered an unsafe shortcut. The independent callback broke the chain.

    Report suspicious messages through your organisation’s process, preserve evidence and independently verify payment changes. A correct training answer is useful practice, not a guarantee against every real scam.
